The compliance reality at Meridian today

Your OFAC screening has one structural blind spot, and it produces regulatory exposure on every list you process.

The blind spot
Name screening cannot see ownership.
Under OFAC's 50 percent rule, Meridian cannot transact with any entity owned at least 50 percent by a sanctioned party, even when the entity's own name never appears on the SDN list. A name screen has no way to see the ownership chain. It waves the entity through. You carry the exposure.
31 CFR §501.801
Example from last quarter's list: Russian Railways. The Russian state controls it through SDN designated parties. Your name screen returns clean.
